INTENTIONAL NOISE / COMMUNITY LOUNGE

Pull up a chair. Make some noise.

Meet other creators using your browser. No client installation required. Public lounge access is experimental while we establish moderation and member accounts.

01 / PUBLIC TEXT

Community text chat

Choose a nickname and open the web IRC client. This currently points to a public, unregistered channel on Libera.Chat, not a private Intentional Noise server. Messages and nicknames are visible to that network.

Opens in a new browser tab. No membership account is created.

02 / VOICE

Voice conversation

Try a browser-based meeting room through Jitsi. Your browser may request microphone access. This is a shared experimental room, not a secure private meeting.

Open voice lounge ↗

A third-party service handles calls. Availability, room controls, and guest access can change.

House rules

Be respectful. No harassment, threats, spam, or posting someone else’s private information. Do not share sensitive business, financial, or personal details in public rooms.

Current status: These are experimental links, not a launched or moderated member community. There is no guaranteed message history, account recovery, access control, or staff monitoring. Registered rooms and private communications come later.
