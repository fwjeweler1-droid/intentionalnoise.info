# Intentional Noise community lounge — v0.5

This release adds lounge.html and links it from community.html. It is a public **experiment**, not a launched private community.

- Text: external Libera.Chat web client, unregistered #intentionalnoise channel. Claim/register the channel and arrange moderation before announcing it broadly. Confirm the IRC client supports the nickname URL parameter; visitors can set nicknames in the client if not.
- Voice: external Jitsi Meet room. Jitsi may require authentication to create rooms, impose limits, or change service terms. Test with two browsers before inviting visitors. Do not represent the room as private or encrypted end-to-end without verification.
- Neither service is operated by Intentional Noise. Review their privacy policies and availability. Do not collect visitor data in this static website.
- The contact form remains disabled pending secure backend configuration.
- Next steps: register and moderate IRC channel, choose hosted or self-hosted IRC/WebSocket gateway, determine voice room access controls, and connect backend for contact forms.
