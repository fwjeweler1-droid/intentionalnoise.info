Privacy Policy

Our commitment across all four connected websites · Updated November 14, 2026

We use member information to operate the services you choose. We do not sell, trade, or provide your personal information to advertisers.

What that means

We do not sell member information, trade it, or build advertising profiles from it. Connect does retain the account, profile, publication, and activity records needed for sign-in and the features members use. Hosting, authentication, and storage providers process information to operate those features. Public artist or booking information appears only through the relevant publication workflow; private member records are not part of the public directory.

What each website handles

IntentionalNoise.info: This informational site serves public pages. Its private contact form is not currently active. Your browser and our hosting provider may process technical request details (such as an IP address) for delivery and security. A local browser preference remembers your chosen light or dark theme.

Connect: Supabase provides sign-in, verified email accounts, and private member records. Artist/musician and instructor profiles, booking intake answers, optional media previews, Studio projects and track records, shared-file references, and member storage shortcuts may be stored when members use those features. A new artist page starts private; member visibility and public artist publication have separate controls and approval steps. Private Studio and intake data are not automatically made public. Members-only resources are available after sign-in.

IneedaGig: Public search and venue-published offers may show the venue, event, location, budget, and other details the poster chose to publish. Private booking intake, account records, and member-only submissions are separate from that public feed. Viewing an offer or submitting interest is not a completed booking, payment, or verification of performance. IneedaGig does not collect booking or performance fees. Optional artist verification payments are handled separately from venue bookings.

IntentionalNoise.band: Artist showcases display publicly approved artist details from Connect. Other site visitors can read what an artist chose to publish.

Communication and shared media

Member communication tools, where available, are operated through service providers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Some messaging and moderation features remain under development or testing; a feature appearing in a development record does not make it available to all members. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, identification documents, medical records, or similarly sensitive information through member conversations.

Members can choose external storage providers such as pCloud, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, or Google Drive for their own files and sharing links. A saved storage preference is a shortcut, not an automatic transfer, backup, or synchronization service. Files or links shared with others are also subject to the chosen provider’s permissions, policies, and charges. Optional external social, chat, and meeting tools have their own terms. Browser translation may run on-device or through the browser provider, depending on its settings; no paid external translator is required to read the site.

Feedback, verification, and future features

Members may voluntarily submit private feedback after joining or resolving a booking request. Feedback is not published as a review. Verified Artist proof requires review of distributor release authorization; it is not proof of copyright ownership. Following approval, an optional one-time $12 payment can secure the member’s existing included free account privileges against later base-tier pricing changes. The account remains free to use before payment. New optional services, including merchant websites and future storage upgrades, are not included in that guarantee. The payment processor, once activated, collects card details on its own hosted checkout; Intentional Noise stores proof status, processor transaction references, amount, payment state, and the associated account-benefit record, not complete card numbers. Payment is never confirmed solely from a return-page link. Other paid features will be separately offered and must be accepted before any charge.

Your choices and questions

Manage profile visibility through Connect member access and manage approved public showcases in the artist page editor. To request a correction, account assistance, or deletion of records, contact Intentional Noise. Removing a published listing does not automatically delete its underlying account, Studio records, external files, previously shared links, or provider logs. We will review account-deletion requests against connected project ownership and retention obligations to avoid unintentionally erasing shared creative work.

This notice describes current functionality as of November 14, 2026. Account assistance and some security, reporting, messaging, and self-service deletion controls remain in development. It does not promise features that have not passed live verification. Last updated November 14, 2026.
