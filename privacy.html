Privacy Policy

Clear terms for messages sent through this website. Updated October 14, 2026.

What we collect

When you contact us, we collect the name, email address, topic, message, and any optional marketing preference you provide. Our infrastructure may process limited technical information needed for security and abuse prevention.

How we use it

We use submissions to respond, coordinate collaborations and services, maintain business records, and protect the website. We send promotional messages only when you separately opt in; you can opt out of marketing at any time.

Storage and retention

We intend to retain business correspondence for future reference, subject to applicable law and reasonable security and retention reviews. Messages are stored in an access-controlled service and may be forwarded to our private business inbox. We do not promise indefinite retention or perfect security.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or disclose it to advertisers. Hosting, message-processing, storage, and email providers may process information to operate the service. We may disclose information in response to legally valid requests, to comply with law, or to protect rights and safety.

Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete information where applicable, or stop marketing messages. Use the website contact form to make a request. Certain records may need to be kept for legal or legitimate business reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The current version will be posted here before any new collection methods go live.
