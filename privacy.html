Privacy Policy

Our commitment across all four connected websites

We don’t save any personal information on our site for distribution in ANY form.

What that means

We do not sell personal information or use it to build advertising profiles. We use third-party services for authentication and essential website functions. Information a member chooses to publish on a public profile or booking listing is visible by that member’s choice.

What each website handles

IntentionalNoise.info: This informational site serves public pages. Its private contact form is not currently active. Your browser and our hosting provider may process technical request details (such as an IP address) for delivery and security. A local browser preference remembers your chosen light or dark theme.

Connect: The member service uses Supabase for authentication and account, artist-profile, and member records. Email verification and sign-in require account data. A new member profile and artist-page draft start private. You separately choose member-list visibility and approve public artist-page publication.

IneedaGig: Public booking listings show offers that a venue publishes, including its chosen venue, location, event, and budget details. Private drafts, applicants' conversations and personal contact details are not part of the public booking feed. Closed or past events should not be presented as open opportunities.

IntentionalNoise.band: Artist showcases display publicly approved artist details from Connect. Other site visitors can read what an artist chose to publish.

Communication and shared media

Our service provider operates the member communication system. Conversations are not end-to-end encrypted; please avoid sharing passwords or sensitive financial, identity, or medical information through it.

Media may be linked from storage providers you choose. Those providers, as well as any optional external social, chat or meeting services you open, have their own privacy policies and costs. No third-party translation service is required to read the site; compatible browsers may provide on-device translation.

Your choices and questions

Manage member visibility through Connect member access and control public artist publication in the artist page editor. To ask about your records, corrections, privacy or account assistance, contact Intentional Noise. Deleting a public listing does not necessarily erase prior private conversation records; ask for assistance rather than assuming instant deletion everywhere.

This notice explains the current website features; security, reporting and account-deletion controls are still being developed. Last updated November 9, 2026.
