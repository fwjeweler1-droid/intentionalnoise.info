04 / HOW WE APPROACH IT

Free to connect.
Fair when it's work.

Participation in the community should not require a recurring membership fee. When professional services or commissioned work are involved, those arrangements are separate, optional, and clearly explained.

We aren't building a business around repeatedly charging creators for vague promises of exposure. Value should be tangible. Agreements should be understandable.

“We don't want your money. We want you to succeed.”

05 / OUR TRANSPARENCY PROMISE

What free
really means.

FREE isn't free. Somebody pays for it. In this case, I do.

I'm a musician, and I'm tired of predatory practices that charge independent creators over and over for vague promises of opportunity. This community exists to help musicians and other creators connect and succeed—not to sell their attention.

Donations are welcome, never required. Nobody needs to buy anything or donate to be welcome in the public community.

Free features depend on available funding, third-party providers, and practical operating limits. They may change, pause, or end. We cannot guarantee uptime, permanent storage, moderation, results, or continued access. We will identify outside services and explain paid options before anyone commits.

How do I make money? Ideally, I don't. Optional professional services are intended to be priced near cost, with a modest margin where needed. Everybody needs to eat, though… so I make Biscuits. More about that later. 😉

“THE COMMUNITY IS NOT THE PRODUCT.”

This is our operating philosophy, not a promise that every feature will remain available forever. See our privacy information and individual service terms for details.
