INTENTIONAL NOISE / OUR PRINCIPLES

Fairness is a working practice.

Our commitments are intended to guide how we build the community and how we approach future professional work.

Creators retain their rights

Ownership and permissions should be stated clearly. Participation alone should not transfer rights to anyone’s work.

Clear terms before commitment

Scope, deliverables, fees, credit, revisions, and rights should be agreed upon before paid work begins.

Respect across disciplines

Musicians, designers, writers, makers, and technical contributors all deserve credit and professional treatment.

No pay-to-belong requirement

We intend basic community participation to be free, without recurring membership charges.

Privacy matters

We do not intend to build an advertising business around community members or their personal information.

Earn trust through results

We would rather make a modest promise and keep it than sell a grand promise we cannot deliver.
